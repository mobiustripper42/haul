  count ended, to issue 130's close on 2026-11-10 13:31 UTC.
- **29.6 active hours** over 9 days with activity, summed from every transcript under
  `~/.claude/projects/-home-eric-haul/`, subagents included, with the rule
  `.claude/skills/build-task/cost.mjs` uses: the gaps between consecutive events shorter than 5
  minutes. The same script over Phase 0's window gives 19.6 h, the figure in the 2026-11-02
  snapshot, so the two are counted the same way. As then, it counts time the session was working or

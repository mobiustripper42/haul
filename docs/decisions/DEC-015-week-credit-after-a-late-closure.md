## DEC-015: Late closures earn a week credit; refunds and store credit stay manual

DEC-005 had the farm refund, by hand in Square, every member charged for a week closed after its
cutoff. Eric, 2026-10-26: a flooded kitchen should not mean fifty refunds — members skip that
week and the next one is on the house. A business folding is still a refund in Square, and no code.

A week credit is not store credit. Store credit is a money balance spent on anything; a week credit
is one box, whatever that week's price or the member's size — a box is a box, for V1. A skipped
week doesn't use it. A member who cancels with one unused is refunded by hand.

Everything else DEC-005 held stands: Haul builds no refund flow and no store-credit balance.

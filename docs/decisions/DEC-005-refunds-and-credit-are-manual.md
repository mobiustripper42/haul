## DEC-005: Refunds and store credit are manual, in Square

Eric's experience in muster (2026-10-25) is that refunds and credit multiply the complexity of a
payment system. Lenny's answer
was that refund requests come to him directly, which fits. The charge run's job is to not charge
what should not be charged — closed and skipped weeks before cutoff — so a refund is the exception.
Both stay in future ideas.

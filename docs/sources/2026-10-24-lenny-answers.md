## Supper Club content (marketing copy)

Let the multicultural kitchen team at Ohio City Farm handle a piece of your busy week's meal prep!
These meals will feature global flavors, simple whole-food ingredients, and seasonal, local produce.
Current members of our kitchen team hail from the Democratic Republic of Congo, Lebanon, Louisiana,
Thailand, and Pakistan, and represent a wide array of culinary knowledge. We are so excited to use
local Ohio-Grown staples to share these flavors with you all.

Sign up for our weekly subscription, starting in January 2027, choose the vegan or non-vegan option,
and choose a portion size, then enjoy your weekly bounty from the farm kitchen team!

Our Rotating menus will include a Family Style Portion of each of the following:

- Protein Main (Non-Vegan or Vegan Option)
- Grain or Bread
- Veggie Side
- Salad
- Soup or Stew

As of right now there will be 2 menus each week, vegan and non-vegan, and we will be limited in our
ability to accommodate allergy requests. All Allergens will be marked on packaging when present, and
please reach out for specific accommodation requests.

Some weeks, we will have a dish that does not keep well for more than a day or two. In this case
(For Example, rice or dressed salad) we will have the dish prepped out for you, with one or two
quick, simple steps to complete it yourself to ensure the meal is fresh and delicious when you eat
it.

## Answers

These are really great questions. I'm going to forward this to Michael and Patrick too so they can
chime in with anything they want to add.

1. I think for refunds people can contact me directly. Cancellations only apply to supper club and
   delivery charges for the CSA since those are the weekly payments. For the CSA, we don't do
   cancellations, all shares that aren't picked up are donated through Re:Source Cleveland's Pantry
   Program for Newcomers.
2. Great Question. I think we would still make their box and if they don't respond to an automatic
   notification to fix it, I would want to reach out to try to get them set up by the time they pick
   up. Worst case scenario we just make an extra box and give it to staff.
3. Pickups will be Sunday and Monday, so at the very least we can still offer the one day, maybe add
   Saturdays too if Monday is off the table. As far as I know, Christmas, New Years, and maybe the
   week of the benefit will be the only weeks we don't do this. It probably makes sense to skip the
   weekend after Thanksgiving too, because everyone will skip that week.
4. No sales tax for Supper club or CSA
5. We've talked about a lot of different versions of this, but I think it could just be a classic
   subscription where you can skip, skipping multiple weeks at once is fine, and you can cancel any
   time. We can always sell a few more subscriptions if we feel like the percentage of skippers is
   too high.
6. I think we need to know our totals by Monday at midnight, because I do a lot of the ordering for
   the kitchen on Tuesdays.
7. The share is donated, along with extra produce from the farm, into Re:source Cleveland's Pantry
   program. This program operates year round bringing dry goods, meat, sanitary products, and
   in-season OCF produce to newcomer families that lost SNAP benefits after new rules were passed
   last year. I don't think we need a donation report from this website, because most people won't
   tell us, they just won't show up to get the share. We keep track of that on the other end when
   the farm crew actually packs the bags.
8. Right now the delivery price is $11.25 per week, but that might go up. We also need to
   re-evaluate how large our zone should be. Supper club is pickup only.
9. Yes we have an email for all of our current members when they buy something online.
10. members.ohiocityfarm.com sounds perfect
11. I think there's very little overlap with our customer base and the newcomer community that
    ReSource serves, but that's a great question and I'll pass it on

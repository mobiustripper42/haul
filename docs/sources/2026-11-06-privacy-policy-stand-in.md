# Privacy Policy

Effective \[EFFECTIVE DATE\]

This policy explains what information the OCF Member Portal at members.ohiocityfarm.com collects and how we use it. The portal is run by Ohio City Farm, a program of \[LEGAL NAME OF RE:SOURCE CLEVELAND\], a nonprofit organization ("OCF," "we," "us"). We collect only what we need to run your subscription or share, and we never sell your information.

## What we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address, phone number and preferred language.
- **Delivery address:** only if you add delivery to a CSA share.
- **Order details:** what you signed up for, your skips and changes, pickups, and your payment history.
- **Payment details:** card payments are handled by Square. We do not see or store your full card number. We keep only what Square returns to us, such as the card brand, the last four digits and the expiry date.
- **Technical details:** a sign-in cookie and the basic records our hosting provider keeps automatically, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the portal secure and working.

## How we use it

- To create your account and sign you in.
- To take payments, run weekly charges, and handle skips, changes and cancellations.
- To prepare pick lists and pickup lists, and to deliver shares.
- To email you about your account and orders, and to send the weekly menu.
- To call or message you about your order when needed, such as a missed pickup or a declined card.
- To keep the portal secure and to understand how our programs are used, in summary form.

## Who we share it with

We do not sell or rent your information, and we do not share it for advertising. We share it only with:

- **Service providers** that run the portal for us, under their own privacy and security terms: Square (payments and customer records), our hosting and database providers, and our email provider. They may use your information only to provide their service to us.
- **Re:Source Cleveland**, the nonprofit that operates Ohio City Farm, for running and reporting on our programs.
- **Authorities**, when the law requires it or to protect the safety of our members, staff or the public.

## Emails

We send account emails, such as sign-in codes, order confirmations and declined-card notices, to every member, because the portal can't work without them. The weekly menu email is optional: you can turn it off with the unsubscribe link in any menu email or in your account.

## Cookies

The portal uses one cookie to keep you signed in. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, limited staff access and a payment processor that meets card-industry security standards. No system is perfectly secure, so we can't guarantee absolute security, but we will notify you if a breach affects your information, as the law requires.

## How long we keep it

We keep your account information while you are a member and for a reasonable time after, so you can come back and so we can answer questions about past orders. We keep payment and tax records for as long as the law requires.

## Children

The portal is for adults. We don't knowingly collect information from children under 13.

## Your choices

- You can see and update your contact details, card and language in your account at any time.
- You can ask us for a copy of your information, or ask us to correct or delete it, by emailing \[CONTACT EMAIL\]. We will delete what we can and tell you what we must keep, such as payment records.
- If you cancel, your account stays so you can rejoin. Ask us if you want it closed.

## Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. If we make a significant change, we will email members before it takes effect. The date at the top shows when the policy last changed.

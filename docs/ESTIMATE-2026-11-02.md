active hours a point**. The range is the two ways Phase 0 grew: its planned rows ran 22% over their
estimates (the low end), and the phase as a whole came to 2.5× its plan once mid-phase additions are
counted (the high end).

## Where the pace comes from

Phase 0, from the retro (`docs/RETROSPECTIVES.md`):

- **67 points** shipped against **27** pokered. The pokered rows, 0.1–0.8, came to 33 (+22%):
  0.3 went 5 → 8 and 0.6 3 → 8 with 0.5's 2 folded into it. The other 34 points were added
  mid-phase as tasks 0.9–0.15. The retro's drift of +8 also counts 0.13's re-estimate, 3 → 5, which
  is inside the 34.
- **9.1 calendar days**, from the first Claude Code activity on 2026-10-24 15:01 UTC to pull
  request #42's merge on 2026-11-02 17:30 UTC (`gh pr view 42 --json mergedAt`). The retro's 7.6
  days starts at the first issue's creation, which leaves out task 0.1 and the planning before it.
- **19.6 active hours** over 10 days with activity, summed from every transcript under
  `~/.claude/projects/-home-eric-haul/`, subagents included, with the rule
  `.claude/skills/build-task/cost.mjs` uses: the gaps between consecutive events shorter than 5
  minutes (`cost.mjs` itself reads one session, so it was not run as is). It counts time the session
  was working or briefly waiting, not keyboard time, and misses work outside Claude Code: mockups in
  Design, review, and messages with the farm. The transcripts live on the developer's machine, so
  this figure cannot be checked from the repository.

**Against the 2026-10-25 estimate:** it put Phase 0 at 27 points and ~9.5 hours at 0.35 h/pt. The
phase took 67 points and 19.6 hours, 0.29 h/pt — inside that estimate's 0.25–0.80 range, near its
low end. The hours ran over because the points did, not the rate.

Weeks

Close a week when there is no pickup. Before its cutoff nobody is charged for it. After its cutoff, everyone who paid gets a week credit: their next box is made and not charged.

Close the week of January 24 and 25?

Nobody has been charged for it. The charge at the end of Monday, January 18 is skipped, and members see the week as closed.

Close the week of January 17 and 18?

31 members were charged for it at the end of Monday, January 11. Each gets a week credit: their next box is made and not charged. This cannot be undone.

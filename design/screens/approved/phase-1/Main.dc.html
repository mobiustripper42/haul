SUPPER CLUB · STARTS JANUARY 2027

Weekly meals from the farm kitchen

Let the multicultural kitchen team at Ohio City Farm handle a piece of your busy week's meal prep. Global flavors, simple whole-food ingredients, and seasonal, local produce grown here in Ohio City.

Our kitchen team comes from the Democratic Republic of Congo, Lebanon, Louisiana, Thailand and Pakistan.

Every week, a family-style portion of each

  • Protein main, standard or vegan
  • Grain or bread
  • Veggie side
  • Salad
  • Soup or stew

Two menus each week, standard and vegan. Allergens are marked on the packaging. Some dishes come with one or two quick steps to finish at home, so they stay fresh.

A SAMPLE WEEK

Shish tawook chicken skewers

Mujadara rice and lentils with onions · Roasted squash · Shredded carrot and walnut salad with sumac · Butternut squash soup

Vegan: Lebanese chickpea moussaka in place of the chicken.
